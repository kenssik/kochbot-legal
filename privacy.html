KochBot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

1. Introduction

KochBot is a Discord bot designed to help protect servers against raids, spam, and suspicious activity.

2. Information Processing

KochBot does not persistently store user or server data. Information may be temporarily processed in memory to provide security features, such as detecting suspicious joins or spam.

3. Data Retention

KochBot is designed not to retain data after temporary processing is complete. Discord and hosting providers may independently process technical information under their own policies.

4. Sharing

KochBot does not sell personal data. The bot communicates with Discord to provide its functionality.

5. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to operate KochBot securely. Absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

6. Your Questions

For privacy questions, contact the KochBot developer through the designated support channel.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated if the bot's functionality or data processing practices change.
